Introduce yourself as an artist 以艺术家的身份介绍自己

The summary of my artist statement: what I make, how I make it, and what I hope it does. 这是我的艺术家自述概要：我做什么、怎么做，以及我希望它起到什么作用。

01 No lessons — only small signs不讲道理，只留线索

I don't tell children what is right or wrong. Instead I leave small signs inside the picture — a shadow, a colour that shifts — so a feeling they cannot name yet becomes something they can actually see. The picture is not an instruction. It is a place where a feeling is allowed to stand in the open. 我不告诉孩子什么是对、什么错。我只是在画面里留下一些小小的暗示——一道影子、一处正在变化的颜色——让那些他们还叫不出名字的情绪，变成看得见的东西。画面不是指令，而是一个让情绪可以被放在明面上的地方。

From the artist statement摘自艺术家自述

02 Soft characters, quiet colours柔和的人物，安静的颜色

Most pages start in Procreate and Photoshop. I keep my characters soft and my colours low. Storyboards, page layout and the look on a face carry the comfort: a drooping shoulder or a toy half-hidden behind a chair often shows worry better than a sentence can. I turn children's vague feelings into faces, bodies and small details, so the reader feels them straight from the picture. 大部分页面从 Procreate 和 Photoshop 开始。我把人物画得柔软，把颜色压得安静。分镜、版面、脸上的表情，共同承担着“被安慰”的感觉：垮下去的肩膀、藏在椅子后面的半个玩偶，常常比一句台词更会说担心。我把孩子模糊的情绪，变成脸、身体和小细节，让读者直接从画面里感觉到它。

Process工作方法

03 A gentle space, not a cure是一个空间，不是一剂解药

My work sits inside children's emotional education. When a child looks at my pictures I don't want them to learn a rule — I want them to find their own feeling in it. Being small and scared isn't a problem to be fixed; it is okay just to sit with it. That is the only thing I am really trying to build: a gentle space where the messy feelings and the mixed ones are allowed to stay. 我的作品属于儿童情绪教育。当孩子看我的画，我不希望他学会一条道理，而是希望他在里面找到自己的情绪。小小的、害怕的，不是一个需要被“治好”的问题——只是待一会儿，也没关系。我真正想搭建的只有一件事：一个温柔的地方，让混乱的、混杂的情绪都可以留下来。

From my Art Manifesto摘自《我的艺术宣言》

Five commandments I draw by我画画时遵守的五条

  1. ⅠSay no to moral lectures. Let conflict, weakness and imperfection exist in art.拒绝道德说教。让冲突、软弱和不完美留在艺术里。
  2. ⅡUse visuals to show feelings — pictures and small details, not only written words.用画面表达情绪——是图像和细节，而不只是文字。
  3. ⅢKeep your true thoughts, and respect the real context.保留真实的想法，也尊重真实的语境。
  4. ⅣValue common daily experiences; you don't need perfect characters.珍视日常的经验；你不需要完美的主角。
  5. ⅤUnderstand what art cannot do. It can comfort, but it cannot replace real human connection.明白艺术做不到什么。它能安慰人，却无法替代真实的人际连接。

“Art shows our inner world, but it cannot fix every problem.” “艺术呈现我们的内心，但它并不能解决所有问题。”

⤢

It's Okay That I Cried我，哭了没关系

Parent handbook家长手册 2026 6 pages · a guide for parents6 页 · 亲子共读指南

A therapeutic picture book for children aged 3–6 and the adults who read with them. The protagonist is an ordinary girl, not a star: she cries when she falls, gets upset over a toy, and doesn't want to let go at the kindergarten gate. The book never says “don't cry”. It follows one feeling from the moment it appears to the moment it settles, and lets it be normal. The handbook was written for the second reader — the adult who often doesn't know what to say. 一本写给 3–6 岁孩子的情绪疗愈绘本，也写给他身边一起读的大人。主角并不是什么小明星，只是一个普通的小女孩：摔倒了会哭，抢玩具会委屈，在幼儿园门口舍不得松手。这本书从不说“不许哭”，它只是陪一种情绪从出现、到顶点、再到平息，让“这样很正常”被说出来。家长手册则写给第二位读者——那位常常不知道该怎么开口的大人。

Little X, ages 3–6小 X 的三到六岁

Character study角色研究 2026 Series development系列角色设定

One girl, four ages, one companion doll. I drew her four times so a single character could carry four different emotional ages: the doll changes size in her arms, her posture straightens, her gaze moves from the floor up to the reader. The doll is the detail that does the work — children recognise their own comfort object before they recognise themselves. 同一个女孩，四个年龄，一只陪伴她的玩偶。我把她画了四遍，让同一个角色承载四种不同的情绪年龄：玩偶在她怀里的大小在变，背脊一点点挺直，视线从地面慢慢抬向读者。真正起作用的是那只玩偶——孩子总是先认出自己的安抚物，再认出自己。

Three — the feeling arrives before the words do.三岁——情绪先到，话还没跟上。 Four — the toy becomes something to hold on to.四岁——玩偶成了可以抓住的东西。 Five — she starts to name what she feels.五岁——她开始给情绪起名字。 Six — she can hold a feeling and still stand up.六岁——她能带着情绪，也仍然站得住。

Part 3 of 3第三部分 / 共三部分

What I actually learned 我真正学到的东西

Four things from this course that changed how I look at my own work — and two things you can play with below. 这门课里有四件事改变了我看自己作品的方式——下面还有两个可以动手玩一玩的部分。

01

Media is not neutral媒介并不中立

Digital media theory taught me that software settings, platform rules and algorithms decide part of what we see. I no longer assume my default brushes, or the style that happens to be trending, are simply “how it looks”. If I follow an algorithm's taste without asking, I am not making a choice — I am inheriting one. 数字媒介理论让我意识到：软件的默认设置、平台规则和算法，都在决定我们看见什么。我不再默认自己的笔刷，或正好流行的风格，就是“它本来的样子”。如果不加追问地跟随算法的口味，我不是在做选择，而是在继承一个选择。

02

The audience is a participant观众是参与者

In Web 1.0 people could only read; in Web 2.0 they comment, share and make their own version. So a piece of visual work is not finished when I export it — it gets finished by the people who respond to it. I design for the response now, not only for the screenshot. Web 1.0 的人只能阅读；Web 2.0 的人会评论、转发，并做出自己的版本。所以一件视觉作品，不是在我导出时就完成的——它由回应它的人完成。我现在为“回应”而设计，而不只是为了那一张好看的截图。

03

Be honest with images对图像保持诚实

Photo editing and AIGC can produce a beautiful picture of something that never happened. That is exactly why the rule stays simple: I cannot fool people with false material. The work can be soft, quiet and emotional — but it cannot be a lie. 修图与 AIGC 可以做出很美、却从未发生过的东西。正因如此，这条规则才要简单：不能用虚假的材料骗人。作品可以柔软、安静、充满情绪——但它不能是谎言。

Three pillars, three jobs in design三个支柱，在设计中各司其职

Rhetoric turned out to be the most practical theory I met this term. Aristotle's Ethos, Pathos and Logos are not only for speeches — each one maps onto a different layer of the things I build. 修辞学是我这学期遇到的最“好用”的理论。亚里士多德的信誉、情感与逻辑，并不只服务于演讲——它们各自对应着我所做的东西的不同层面。

Understanding the taste of the audience, and letting the image reach it. In a game UI it is the colour, the weight, the pause before the reward. In a picture book it is the low saturation and the small, quiet detail — the feeling is persuaded, never told.理解观众的口味，并让图像抵达它。在游戏界面里，它是颜色、重量、奖励出现前的那半秒停顿；在绘本里，它是低饱和的颜色和安静的小细节——情绪是被“劝服”的，而不是被告知的。

In my work在我的作品里

The rules and the feedback loop. When a button behaves the way a player expects, the interface is quietly making a logical argument: this world follows laws, so you can trust your own decisions in it.规则与反馈闭环。当一个按钮的反应正如玩家所料，界面其实在安静地做一次逻辑论证：这个世界有其法则，所以你可以相信自己的判断。

In my work在我的作品里

Consistent IP visuals and brand materials. If the same world looks like the same world every time it appears, people trust it — and trust is what lets a soft, quiet image be taken seriously in a commercial channel.统一的 IP 视觉与品牌物料。如果同一个世界每次出现都还是那个世界，人们就会相信它——而正是这种信任，让一张安静的、柔软的画面，在商业渠道里也能被认真对待。

Drag the marker — or pick a name. My answer never settles at either end. 拖动标记，或者点一个名字。我的答案从不停在两端。

my position我的位置

Baudelaire & Zola, 19th century波德莱尔与左拉，19世纪

Only personal taste: “I like it” or “I don't.” No history, no theory, no context. Criticism becomes a mood — and a mood cannot be argued with.只凭个人喜好：“我喜欢”或“我不喜欢”。不谈历史、不谈理论、不谈语境。批评于是变成一种心情——而心情是无法被讨论的。

Diderot, the father of modern art criticism, wrote the Salon reviews from his own point of view. What he published was a personal opinion — and that public, subjective voice became the counter-weight to the royal academy's rigid rules.现代艺术批评之父狄德罗，写沙龙评论时本身就带着自己的视角。他发表的是一种私人意见——而正是这种公众的、主观的声音，成为了制衡皇家学院僵化教条的砝码。

Baudelaire and Zola took sides for the Impressionists before the academy did. Clear positions, not neutrality — criticism as advocacy. It is not objective, and it still changed what counted as art.在学院承认之前，波德莱尔与左拉就主动为印象派站队。是鲜明立场，而非中立——批评成为一种辩护。它并不客观，却确实改变了什么算作艺术。

Formalist criticism built a theoretical frame — flatness, pure colour, the properties of the medium — so that judgement would not collapse back into liking and disliking. Full subjectivity is stopped here, but somebody still chose what to look at.形式主义批评搭起一套理论框架——平面性、色彩的纯粹性、媒介自身的特质——让判断不至于又滑回“喜欢 / 不喜欢”。完全的主观在这里被拦住了，但“看什么”仍然是有人在选。

Institutional critique uses academic theory to examine Eurocentrism and systemic bias inside art institutions. The tools are shared and the evidence matters — but the choice of which wall to look at is still made by a person.机构批判借助学术理论，剖析艺术机构内部的欧洲中心主义与结构性偏见。工具是共享的，证据是重要的——但“去看哪一面墙”，仍然是人做出来的选择。

Good criticism includes the feeling the work produced in me — then puts that feeling on a ground of art history, formal analysis, social context and theory. Subjectivity gives criticism its energy. Objectivity gives it something to argue with. 好的艺术批评，既包含作品在我身上引起的主观感受，也把这份感受安放在艺术史、形式分析、社会语境与理论之上。主观给批评以活力，客观给批评以可讨论的依据。
